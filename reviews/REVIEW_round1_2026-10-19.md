## 5. Consensus / complementarity / disagreement

**Consensus (all or most reviewers):** the computational core is trustworthy — every quoted number reproduces exactly from raw counts; the E1 secondary endpoint is robust and its selectivity claim is *stronger* than the paper claims when tested properly (P = 0.0017); the primary-endpoint null is honest but its "correctly-powered" framing is not; the packaging layer (BH provenance, manifests, tag, docx figures) contradicts the manuscript's own claims; "pre-registered" must be reworded.

**Complementarity:** A1 found what only a domain expert finds (the inverted mechanism, the model identity, the detection-floor gene); A2 found what only a statistician finds (the interaction logic, exact enumeration, MDE); A3 found what only an auditor finds (stale manifests, dead builder code, mislabeled keys); A4 found what only the venue finds (word limits, declarations, citation style). No layer's findings could have been produced by another.

**Disagreements and adjudication:**

1. **Pre-registration timeline (A1 lenient vs A3/A4 strict).** A1 noted the panel JSON (09:22:16) predates the count downloads (09:22:29/09:24:56) and called the timeline "compatible with the claim"; A3 noted the protocol *document* (09:34) postdates the first results (09:26); A4 called the panel write "interleaved with the data download". **Ruling:** A4/A3's stricter reading governs, because "pre-registered" is a term of art requiring an *external, tamper-evident* time-stamp preceding data examination; panel-before-counts does not establish protocol-before-examination, and neither is externally timestamped. Adopt "prospectively specified". (A1's leniency certifies a narrower claim — that the panel file predates the counts — which the revised text may state factually without the term of art.)
2. **Power claim (A3 "remove" vs A2 "quantify").** A3 recommended replacing "correctly-powered" with a percentile-only statement; A2 supplied the MDE/CI quantification. **Ruling:** adopt A2's quantified bounded-null (it preserves the honest core — the null excludes large shifts — that A3's version discards), but drop the words "correctly-powered" everywhere, as A1/A3/A4 unanimously require. Verified by the editor: MDE 0.179 (exact-t) / 0.164 (normal-approx) vs observed 0.049; CI [−0.176, +0.079] (Welch, editor-recomputed, identical to A2's).
3. **Permutation inference (A2 exact enumeration).** The manuscript's MC values are reproducible under the deposited seed but deviate from exact enumeration in load-bearing places. **Ruling:** adopt exact enumeration (3432/924/792 permutations — zero cost), update all quoted values, drop the 1/B floor. Editor independently enumerated and obtained identical values.
4. **N3 status (A1 "genuine co-finding" vs A2 "positive control, not neutral").** Not a true conflict: both are right. **Ruling:** re-label N3 as an exposure (positive) control *and* keep the biosynthetic-response interpretation as a co-finding, explicitly.
5. **Gene-level testing (A2 option (a) framing sentence vs (b) BH within panel).** **Ruling:** adopt (a) — keep the pre-registered framing (module level = confirmatory; gene level = descriptive) and add the framing sentence; do not introduce a new within-panel BH layer that was not pre-specified. The numbers are already correct; only the tone needs repair.

## 7. What stands up (do NOT change these)

5. **The ethics position is substantively correct** for a public-data reanalysis (no new human-subject research); it merely needs to be stated inside the manuscript, not only in the cover letter.
6. **The donor-confounding safeguard** (HZ-vs-control as primary; PHN contrast exploratory) is the right design and is honestly reported.
7. **The reference infrastructure** (repo, tag, MANIFEST mechanism, gate) exists and is the right architecture — it is out of date, not wrong.

**Path A — restructure and resubmit as the same article type (recommended).** The strongest real finding — route-dependent, channel-class-selective suppression of the electrogenic nociceptor programme by acute-HZ serum exosomes, with an interaction test at P = 0.0017 and a correctly bounded null on the stiffness programme — is currently *buried under* a fragile allodynia-mechanism claim that the causal literature contradicts. Swapping the headline (from "substrate for allodynia" to "channel-class selectivity + bounded null") converts the paper from a fragile signal into a stable, defensible finding. All Tier 0–3 items are editorial/analytical, not experimental; one round of revision suffices. Article type unchanged.

**Path B (downgrade article type) — not recommended.** The finding supports Original Research.

**Path C (wording-only) — not viable.** Tier 0–1 items change numbers (exact enumeration, MDE/CI, interaction tests, sensitivity) and artefacts (manifests, tag, docx), not just words.

## 9. Process lessons (what the gates could not catch — and what would extend coverage)

1. **A consistency gate verifies strings it enumerates; both false claims (M4 range, module completeness) sat in sentences the gate never asserted.** Extension: the gate must assert *every* quantitative claim in the manuscript, generated from the results artefact — or state its coverage explicitly. Added in revision.
2. **A gate passing at 100% is evidence about arithmetic, not design.** The numbers were perfect and the *interpretation* (allodynia substrate, dissociation, "correctly-powered") was wrong in three places. Design-layer review by an independent panel is not optional for this author's workflow.
3. **"Regenerates every number" is a compound claim: it covers only what the deposited scripts produce.** The `bh_q` block was correct but unregenerable — the most dangerous possible state, because it passes every numerical check while failing the one reproducibility test a reviewer will actually run. Extension: the revision's gate now re-runs the full pipeline into a scratch directory and diffs against the deposited JSON.
4. **The second occurrence is the one that goes stale.** Manuscript hashes in the root MANIFEST pointed at a third version of the manuscript (neither tag nor submission). Extension: one canonical version string; manifest regenerated from final files at release time only.
5. **A "frozen" panel and a "pre-registered" protocol are only as good as their external time-stamps.** On-disk mtimes and same-day git commits cannot establish precedence. The honest wording ("prospectively specified … publicly archived") preserves the methodological strength without borrowing a term of art the evidence does not support.
6. **Significant-vs-not-significant is still the most common way to claim an interaction.** The corrected test *strengthened* the paper's real claim (P = 0.002) while removing a false one — the revision is net-positive precisely because the correct analysis was run.

Your strongest claim that does not hold is the "channel imbalance = allodynia substrate" framing — and its failure is not your fault: the mechanosensation literature it should cite (Piezo2 gain-of-function in allodynia; SCN9A loss-of-function in analgesia) is not part of the standard bioinformatics reading list, and the aggregate module-score layer hides that allodynia is a gain phenomenon. The finding you buried is better: **acute-HZ serum exosomes selectively suppress the electrogenic nociceptor programme over the mechanogated one — interaction P = 0.0017 — while the ECM–Hippo stiffness programme is inert within a bounded null (MDE 0.18 log2CPM).** Swapping the two converts the paper from a fragile signal into a stable structural finding, and it costs you nothing: every number you need is already computed.
